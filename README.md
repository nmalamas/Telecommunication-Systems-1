# Telecommunication Systems I

MATLAB exercises and reports for the **Telecommunication Systems I** course at the Technical University of Crete (2023–24).

## Exercises

> [Exercise 1](Exercise%201) - **SRRC Pulses and 2-PAM.** Autocorrelation of simple pulses, and the spectrum and bandwidth of truncated Square Root Raised Cosine (SRRC) pulses. A check that SRRC pulses are orthonormal, and a simulation of a baseband 2-PAM system.

> [Exercise 2](Exercise%202) - **Spectral Analysis of PAM Waveforms.** Energy spectral density of SRRC pulses, and periodograms of baseband 2-PAM waveforms. The experimental PSD (averaged over many realizations) is compared with the theoretical one.

> [Exercise 3](Exercise%203) - **16-QAM System Simulation.** A complete 16-QAM transmitter and receiver, built as two 4-PAM branches with Gray coding, SRRC filters, carrier modulation and an AWGN channel. Symbol and bit error probabilities are estimated with Monte Carlo simulation and compared with theory.
